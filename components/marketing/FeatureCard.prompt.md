A full-colour panel for a top-level proposition — the studio uses exactly two, side by side, to split its work into open data and commissioned solutions.

```jsx
<FeatureCard
  title="Open Data Projects"
  image={dashboardPhoto}
  action={<Button variant="inverse">View Open Data</Button>}
>
  Free, public resources that solve critical data gaps and showcase our
  capabilities (self-funded or grant funded)
</FeatureCard>
```

The photograph is decoration, not information — keep it at 30% under the veil so type contrast never depends on the image. Pair with an `inverse` button; a filled blue button on blue is wrong.
