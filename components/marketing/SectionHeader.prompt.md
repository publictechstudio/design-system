Opens every marketing section — 48px bold, centred, with a muted lede.

```jsx
<SectionHeader
  title="Our Services"
  lede="We can either execute the entire project, from data collection to product
        development, or support your team with specific services or hands-on training."
/>
```

Section titles are the one place the studio uses Title Case ("Open Data Projects", "Get In Touch", "About Us"); everything else is sentence case. Keep them to two or three words — the lede does the explaining.
