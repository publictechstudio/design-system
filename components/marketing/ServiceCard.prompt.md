A capability the studio sells — six of them run in one row on wide screens, so keep titles short and descriptions to one clause.

```jsx
<ServiceCard icon={<ClipboardList size={32} />} title="Data Collection">
  From scoping projects, to designing data schemas and survey forms, to
  conducting field work and quality checks
</ServiceCard>
```

Every cap is the same blue — do not colour-code services. Descriptions end without a full stop, which is deliberate and consistent across all six.
